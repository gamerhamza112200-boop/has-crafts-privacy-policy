HAS Crafts Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

HAS Crafts operates the HAS Crafts Auto Pinner application. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when the application is used to connect with Pinterest.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the application through Pinterest, the application may access information necessary to manage the authenticated Pinterest account, including the user's Pinterest boards and Pins, according to the permissions granted through Pinterest OAuth.

2. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through Pinterest is used only to provide the application's intended functionality, including organizing and publishing Pins to the authenticated user's Pinterest boards.

3. Pinterest Authentication

The application uses Pinterest's official authorization system. We do not ask users to provide their Pinterest password directly to the application.

4. Data Storage

HAS Crafts does not sell personal information or use Pinterest account information for advertising purposes. Authentication information is handled only as necessary for the application's Pinterest functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

The application uses Pinterest's official API and services. Pinterest may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information used by the application. Users should keep their authentication credentials and access tokens private.

7. Data Deletion

Users can revoke the application's access through their Pinterest account settings. If you need assistance with application-related data deletion, please contact HAS Crafts.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.
